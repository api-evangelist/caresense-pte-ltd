# CareSense Pte. Ltd.

> Singapore-based preventive digital health company (CareSense.ai) building AI-powered health coaching. Its flagship product is Mandy, a 24/7 AI health coach delivered over chat and voice, paired with the CareSense.ai app — a personal health dashboard covering nutrition, exercise, sleep and stress — and integrated health services including blood testing and telemedicine. Sold to individuals and to enterprise teams as a workplace wellbeing programme.

CareSense Pte. Ltd. was incorporated in Singapore on 14 June 2024 (UEN 202424008M) at 160 Robinson Road #14-04. It was founded by Lucas Ngoo, co-founder of Carousell, and Julian Koo, founder of the home-care platform Jaga-Me.

**No public API.** As of 2026-07-20 CareSense publishes no public API, developer portal, API reference, OpenAPI description or SDKs. The host `api.caresense.ai` resolves and terminates TLS but returns 404 on every probed path; `developer.caresense.ai` and `docs.caresense.ai` do not resolve. Do not assume programmatic access exists — contact the company directly.

**Not to be confused with** CareSense by MedTrak Inc. (caresense.com), an unrelated US digital care-navigation product.

## Products
- [Mandy — AI Health Coach](https://www.caresense.ai/): 24/7 personalized AI health coaching over chat and voice
- [CareSense.ai web app](https://web.caresense.ai/app): personal health dashboard
- [CareSense.ai for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.caresenseai.caresenseai): mobile app
- [Clarity](https://clarity.caresense.ai/): CareSense platform surface
- Integrated health services: blood testing, telemedicine and connected care
